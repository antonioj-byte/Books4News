# Escenarios de la ilustración de «Cinco del libro»

Una ilustración al día (2/11, elegida por el usuario: la dirección «a sangre» con la mesa del día): cinco objetos en una sola fila de izquierda a derecha, uno por noticia y en el orden del carrusel. La portada la enseña entera y cada noticia hace zoom a su objeto, así que la fila y las posiciones son fijas: lo dice la sección «fila», que el código añade a todas. Un escenario por día de la semana (lunes es la primera sección), para que no se repita en la semana. El código (`folio.cinco`) pone los cinco objetos donde dice `{objetos}` y añade el estilo de la casa (`prompts/ilustracion_editorial.md`). Lo que hay antes de las secciones no se envía.

## fila

The five objects stand in one single row across the middle of the picture, evenly spaced, at one tenth, three tenths, one half, seven tenths and nine tenths of the width, each clearly separate and about the same size, with empty space around it. One single continuous painting, with no borders, gutters or dividing lines. Every surface is blank: no letters, numbers, labels, engravings or logos anywhere, and nothing written on any object, sign or glass.

## mesa

Seen from directly above, a dark wooden editor's desk at dawn, with soft window light; on it, from left to right: {objetos}.

## balda

A long wooden bookshelf seen straight on, its shelf of plain spines interrupted by five objects standing on it, from left to right: {objetos}.

## escaparate

The display window of a small bookshop at night, seen from the street through the plain glass, warm light inside, with no shop sign; on the low display platform, from left to right: {objetos}.

## corcho

A large cork board on a wall, seen straight on, with five objects pinned to it with coloured pins and joined by a red thread, from left to right: {objetos}.

## tendedero

A clothesline across a rooftop terrace at golden hour, seen straight on, with five objects hanging from it by wooden pegs, from left to right: {objetos}.

## vitrina

A quiet museum gallery with five low white plinths in a row, each holding one object under soft spotlights, from left to right: {objetos}.

## carrito

A wooden library cart in a long library corridor, seen from the side, its top shelf holding five objects, from left to right: {objetos}.
